Delete your account & data

Fade  ·  Last updated: 16 July 2026

Before you publish: confirm the contact email, and make sure the in-app “Delete account” action actually exists and performs the deletion described here.

You can delete your Fade account and the personal data linked to it at any time. There are two ways:

Option A — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Fade and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and personal data are removed.

Option B — By email

Send a request to privacy@fade.uz from the email or with the phone number on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We may ask one question to confirm it is really you before we delete.

What gets deleted

DeletedMay be retained
Your name, phone number, email, profile and shop photos, bookings, messages, reviews, and account settings. Limited records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons (for example, a record that a fee was charged), kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted.

AI hairstyle preview photos are not retained, so there is nothing separate to delete for those.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate for your account. Any backups are cleared within 30 days.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Fade mobile application (“Fade”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Fade is operated by Fade in Uzbekistan. By using Fade you agree to this Policy.

2. How we collect it

  • Telegram login: when you choose “Continue with Telegram”, Telegram shares the phone number it has already verified for you, and your Telegram display name. We never see your Telegram password.
  • Google Sign-In: Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Directly from you: details you type, photos you add, and permissions you grant (location, camera).

3. The AI hairstyle preview

If you use the AI hairstyle preview, the selfie you choose is sent to a third-party AI image service to generate the preview. We ask for your explicit consent the first time before anything is sent, and we use that photo only to create your preview — we don’t add it to your profile or show it to barbers or other users, and we don’t use it for any other purpose. The third-party service processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control. If you choose not to consent, nothing is sent from your device and the rest of the app works normally.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data or share it for others’ advertising. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Fade:

  • Supabase — our secure database, authentication, and hosting provider.
  • Telegram — to verify your phone number at login.
  • Google — to authenticate you if you use Google Sign-In.
  • The SMS provider — if you sign in with an SMS code, your phone number is shared with our SMS gateway solely to deliver that verification code.
  • The AI image service — only the selfie you submit for a preview, and only with your consent (see section 3).
  • A barber you book — sees your name and the phone number needed to contact you about that booking. Your number is not shown publicly or to other clients.

Fade’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use data received from Google only to sign you in and provision your account.

5. Barbers are independent recipients

Fade is a platform that connects clients and barbers. When you book, the barber receives the contact details needed to carry out that appointment. Our Terms require barbers to use your details only to carry out your booking, keep them secure, and not use them for marketing or anything else without your consent. From that point the barber decides independently how they use those details and is separately responsible, as an independent controller, for doing so under applicable law. We do not control, and are not responsible for, a barber’s handling of your information, their conduct, or the service they provide. Complaints about a barber should be raised with that barber; you may also report them to us using the contact address below.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Following a deletion request we remove your personal data from our live systems within a reasonable period, ordinarily 30 days. Residual copies may persist in routine encrypted backups for a further limited period until those backups are overwritten in the normal cycle. We retain whatever we are required to retain for legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution or fraud-prevention purposes, and we may keep aggregated or anonymised data that no longer identifies you.

7. How we keep your data safe

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access rules that, in normal operation, keep you to your own data and the public parts of the marketplace. As with any online service, no method of transmission or storage can be perfectly secure, and no online service can guarantee absolute security. Keeping your device and your sign-in account (Google or Telegram) secure is your responsibility.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To delete your account and data, use the in-app option or the process described at fade.uz/delete, or contact us at the address below. We may ask you to verify your identity before acting, and we may decline or charge a reasonable fee for requests that are manifestly unfounded, repetitive or excessive. We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law. Because a phone number in Uzbekistan is linked to identity documents, we treat your number as sensitive and limit who can see it.

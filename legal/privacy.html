2. How we collect it

  • Telegram login: when you choose “Continue with Telegram”, Telegram shares the phone number it has already verified for you, and your Telegram display name. We never see your Telegram password.
  • Google Sign-In: Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Directly from you: details you type, photos you add, and permissions you grant (location, camera).

3. The AI hairstyle preview

If you use the AI hairstyle preview, the selfie you choose is sent to an AI image service to generate the preview. We ask for your explicit consent the first time before anything is sent. The photo is used only to create that single preview, is not shown to barbers or other users, and is not retained by us after the preview is generated. If you do not consent, no photo leaves your device and the rest of the app works normally.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Fade:

  • Supabase — our secure database, authentication, and hosting provider.
  • Telegram — to verify your phone number at login.
  • Google — to authenticate you if you use Google Sign-In.
  • The AI image service — only the selfie you submit for a preview, and only with your consent (see section 3).
  • A barber you book — sees your name and the phone number needed to contact you about that booking. Your number is not shown publicly or to other clients.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account we remove your personal data, except anything we must keep for legal or accounting reasons. AI preview photos are not retained after the preview is generated.

6. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security so that, in normal operation, you can only read your own data and the public parts of the marketplace. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal data. To delete your account and data, use the in-app option or the process described at fade.uz/delete, or contact us at the address below. Because a phone number in Uzbekistan is linked to identity documents, we treat your number as sensitive and limit who can see it.

8. Children

Fade is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.

Questions or requests: privacy@fade.uz.

Fade is the fastest way to book a great haircut in Uzbekistan.

Find barbers and barbershops near you, see real prices and reviews, pick a time,

WHY FADE
• Discover — browse barbershops on a live map with photos, prices and ratings.
• Book in seconds — pick a barber, a service and a time. Done.
• No more waiting — your slot is reserved; show your booking QR at the chair.
• Earn Fade Points — get points back on every completed cut and spend them on
  your next visit.
• Trusted status — regulars unlock instant booking, no deposit, and priority.
• Invite friends — share your code and earn points when they get their first cut.
• Try before you sit — preview hairstyles with AI (fully on your device).

FAIR FOR EVERYONE
Fade connects clients with independent barbers. Prices are set by the barbers,
shown up front, with no hidden fees. Reviews are honest — dual-rated for the
barber's skill and the shop's vibe.

FOR BARBERS
Run your chair from your phone: accept bookings, manage your day, protect your
time from no-shows, and grow a loyal client base. Getting started is free.

SIGN IN SAFELY
Continue with Telegram or Google — no passwords to remember. Your phone number
is only ever used to confirm your booking.

Book your next cut on Fade.
